        How the rules work here

        Boards. Three 4×4 main levels (White, Neutral, Black) overlap by two ranks. Four 2×2 attack boards sit on pegs at the corners. Files run z, a–d, e; ranks run 0–9.

        Moving pieces. Every piece moves as in ordinary chess, judged on the flattened top-down view, and may land on that square on any level that has it. A path is blocked if a square along it is occupied on any level. Knights jump.

        Pawns move forward one (two on their first move), capture diagonally forward, and become queens when no square lies ahead of them.

        Attack boards. Instead of moving a piece, you may move an attack board that is empty and yours, or holds just one of your pieces. It moves to a free peg on the same side, either the other end of the same level or the same end of the next level, carrying its piece.

        Check, checkmate and stalemate work as usual. Castling and en passant are left out.
